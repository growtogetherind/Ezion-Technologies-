# Ezion Technologies & Ezion Media — Website Simplification

Streamlined, high-converting, premium website designed for **Swapnil Togare**, **Ezion Technologies**, and **Ezion Media**.

## Strategic Architecture

The site makes Ezion easy to understand in **10 seconds**, presenting two clear operational divisions and an offshore delivery engine:
1. **Ezion Technologies ("We build")**: Websites, SaaS products, custom software, mobile apps, and custom AI agents.
2. **Ezion Media ("We grow")**: Lead generation, Google & Meta Ads, ad copy and creatives, and branding & marketing.
3. **Offshore Delivery Partner**: Dedicated teams, white-label delivery, and project-based outsourcing from an established India delivery centre with Canadian advisory presence.

## Brand Design System
- **Corporate Base Navy**: `#0B2545`
- **Ezion Technologies Accent (Teal)**: `#13A89E` / `#7DD3D0` / `#E8F8F7`
- **Ezion Media Accent (Amber)**: `#F59E0B` / `#D97706` / `#FFFBEB`
- **Typography**: [Outfit](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Outfit) for headlines & branding + [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) for clean UI & body copy.
- **Visual Identity**: Clean overlapping circle brand motifs with division-specific SVG marks (`logo.svg`, `logo-media.svg`, and `logo-ezion.svg`).

## Page Structure

### 1. Homepage (`index.html`)
Follows the exact 8-section simplified flow:
1. **Hero**: Headline *"Software that works. Marketing that grows."*, sub-line, CTAs (*Talk to us* / *See our services*), and *"Canada presence · India delivery team"*.
2. **Two Division Cards**: Side-by-side presentation of *Ezion Technologies* ("We build") and *Ezion Media* ("We grow").
3. **Technologies Services**: 5 clean cards (*Website design & development*, *SaaS development*, *Software development*, *App development*, *AI agents & AI development*) with interactive *Enquire* links that auto-select the service on the contact form.
4. **Media Services**: 4 clean cards (*Lead generation*, *Google Ads & Meta Ads*, *Ad copy & creatives*, *Branding & marketing*) plus the AI enquiry shortlisting banner and link to the detailed services page.
5. **Partner with Us (Offshore Team)**: 3 tiles (*Dedicated team*, *White-label*, *Project outsourcing*), *"Need a reliable team in India?"*, and partnership CTAs.
6. **Why Ezion**: 3 focused points (*One team*, *Clear communication*, *Canada presence, India delivery*) with 4 visible trust signals (*Strict Mutual NDA*, *100% IP Ownership*, *Time-Zone Overlap*, *Transparent Terms*).
7. **How We Work**: 4-step execution timeline (*Discover · Plan · Build or launch · Review and support*).
8. **Contact**: Operations credibility (Canada: Vivek Jain, India: Swapnil B. Togare & Neeta B. Togare, real phone lines, unified `@eziontechnologies.com` domain) and a single form with the 7 specified service options.
- **Footer**: Unified logo, 5 menu items, direct phone/email channels, legal links, and link to `/services.html`.

### 2. Detailed Services Page (`services.html`)
Comprehensive deep-dive page holding all secondary detail without cluttering the homepage:
1. **Ezion Technologies in Detail** (7 services with Title, 2-line copy, *"Who it is for"*, and *"What the client receives"*).
2. **Ezion Media in Detail** (6 services with Title, 2-line copy, *"Who it is for"*, and *"What the client receives"*).
3. **Partner with Us** (6 offshore capabilities: dedicated pod, white-label, fixed project, data/research ops, creative production, and back-office).
4. **How We Work with You** (3 commercial engagement models: *Fixed project*, *Monthly retainer*, *Dedicated resource*).
5. **Where We Work** (5 global hubs: Canada, USA, UK, UAE, and India delivery centre).
6. **Request a Custom Proposal** (Comprehensive proposal builder form with multi-service checklist).
